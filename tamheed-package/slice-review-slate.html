The requirement — FR-165

As stored

As a Chairman or Secretary, I want to open the session view of a presenter I choose, targeted by meeting and agenda topic, so that I can see what an external presenter will actually be shown before the meeting rather than inferring it. The preview shall render the SAME composition the targeted presenter would see - the access banner carrying that person's own stored expiry, the topic card, the agenda-slot card with its position and time box, and the list of materials attached to the slot's topic - and shall reproduce that person's EMPTY STATES as faithfully as it reproduces their content, so a preview of a cancelled meeting, or of an agenda item with no presenter assigned, shows nothing rather than showing more than the presenter would get. Materials shall be LISTED and shall NOT be openable from the preview; retrieval of an attachment by a committee member is a separate capability that does not exist anywhere in the product today. The targeting parameter shall be accepted ONLY from Chairman and Secretary: every other principal shall be refused, and a Guest attempting it shall be refused as firmly as a Secretary is served, with the refusal proven by forcing it rather than by asserting that it was not attempted. A successful preview shall itself be recorded in the audit trail, naming the caller and the targeted meeting and topic, because one principal reading a view scoped to another person is a governance act and not an ordinary read. The entry point shall be reached from the agenda surface where the presenter is already named, and shall NOT be a navigation item: the guest-only navigation access map is unchanged.
