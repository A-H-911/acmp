A GUEST PRESENTER CAN READ EVERY MEETING IN THE COMMITTEE, NOT ONLY THE ONE THEY PRESENT AT - so AC-011's 'or any action outside that meeting scope returns 403' is UNMET on its second clause. Found 2026-08-15 while scoping the AC-011 live leg, by reading the guest enforcement path before writing the test rather than after it failed. FOUND BY READING, NOT YET BY MEASUREMENT - the e2e leg being built will confirm or refute it, and this row is to be corrected rather than defended if the measurement disagrees. THE READING, three independent points that agree: (1) GuestSurfaceMiddleware is deny-by-default for a Guest-only principal, but its allowlist admits GET on the whole /api/meetings prefix unconditionally - the constant is ReadOnlyMeetings and the test is StartsWithSegments plus HttpMethods.IsGet, with no comparison against which meeting the caller actually presents at. (2) MeetingsEndpoints maps GET / and GET /{key} on a bare RequireAuthorization() group, and its own comment reads 'Reads - any authenticated committee member (committee-wide read)' - but a Guest is NOT a committee member, and the middleware is what lets them reach a route whose comment assumes they cannot. (3) GetMeetingsQuery and GetMeetingDetailQuery apply no caller scoping: neither appears among the Meetings handlers that inject ICurrentUser, and neither mentions Guest. The pipeline order in Program.cs is UseAuthentication then UseAcmpGuestSurface then UseAuthorization, so nothing downstream re-scopes the read. WHY THIS IS A GAP AND NOT THE DESIGN: DEF-052's own record says the allowlist 'is not a new judgement' because the design's role matrix gives Guest 'agenda (view)'. The defensible reading of that matrix is that a guest views THE agenda - their own slot's, which /session already renders caller-scoped by construction - not that they may browse every agenda the committee has ever scheduled. ADR-0040's stated purpose is that FR-159 creates the first EXTERNAL principal and that unpublished committee material must not become readable by someone outside the organisation; a committee-wide meeting and agenda list is exactly that material at metadata level - every meeting's key, title, schedule and agenda item topic titles. GuestSurfaceMiddleware's own summary line is 'a Guest reaches the guest surface and NOTHING ELSE', which the meetings entry contradicts in spirit. WHAT IS CORRECTLY ENFORCED, and it is most of the AC: a Guest-only principal is refused /api/topics entirely, so AC-011's PRIMARY clause - attempting to access a different topic returns 403 - holds, with reason guest_scope. /api/session cannot express a request for somebody else's slot (there is no parameter naming a meeting, topic or person), and the material download re-derives the topic from the caller's own slots and fails closed. So this is the SECOND clause only. BLAST RADIUS TODAY IS EFFECTIVELY NIL BUT NOT ZERO, and the distinction matters: a guest can only exist where KeycloakAdmin:Enabled is true, which is false in CI and dev but was set TRUE on prod and UAT on 2026-08-12 (OQ-075, AV-145). Production is unused - zero topics, one of 26 members has ever signed in - so there is no committee data to expose and no guest has been invited. This is therefore a latent authorization gap rather than an incident, the same framing DEF-065 carries. FIX OPTIONS, needing a decision rather than a reflex: (a) scope the guest's meeting read to meetings they present at, which is the reading AC-011 states and which /session already computes - the predicate exists in GetMySessionHandler and would move to a shared place; (b) drop /api/meetings from the guest allowlist entirely and let /session be the only meeting surface a guest has, which is simpler and matches 'the guest surface and NOTHING ELSE' but removes a read the design's matrix appears to grant; (c) amend AC-011 to say the presenter relationship is scoped to the assigned TOPIC, accepting committee-wide agenda visibility as intended - which needs a scope-change row and should not be chosen merely because it is the cheapest way to make the AC green. Do NOT close this by evidencing AC-011 against the topic clause alone while leaving the second clause unmentioned.
