ASSIGN THE GUEST REALM ROLE ON THE FR-159 INVITE PATH ONLY, making the two invite paths deliberately asymmetric. RECORDED BEFORE THE CHANGE IS WRITTEN. TODAY both FR-156's InviteUser and FR-159's GuestProvisioner run one shared body, MemberInvitation.InviteAsync, which calls identity.CreateUserAsync and never assigns a realm role - so every invited account, guest or not, exists in Keycloak with no role. For FR-156 that is DELIBERATE: an invited member is inert until an administrator assigns a role through AssignRoles, which is ADR-0038's two-step flow. For FR-159 it is a defect (DEF-076): the guest presenter's token then satisfies nothing, GetMySessionQuery refuses them from their own session, and GuestSurfaceMiddleware does not even classify them as a guest because IsGuestOnly reads the token. THE CHANGE: the guest path assigns the Guest realm role as part of provisioning the guest, so the Secretary's single action from the meeting screen produces a usable presenter, per DEC-037. The FR-156 path is UNCHANGED and keeps creating an inert account. WHY THIS IS A SCOPE CHANGE AND NOT MERELY A BUG FIX: it makes two paths that deliberately shared one body behave differently, and it moves a role assignment from an administrator's explicit decision into an automatic consequence of a Secretary's invite - a narrow but real widening of what the Secretary role causes to happen in the identity provider. ADR-0040 already grants the Secretary that authority in substance (the whole use case is handing an external person scoped read access), so this implements the ADR rather than extending it; recording it here is so the asymmetry is a decision on the record rather than an artefact somebody later 'tidies up' by re-merging the two paths.
